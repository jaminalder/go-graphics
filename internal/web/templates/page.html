Favourites can be kept in this browser. They are recipes for images, so they take very little space. They are not an account or a permanent online collection.

{{else if eq .Kind "about"}}

Made with a little chance.

This studio creates original generative images from deterministic artwork algorithms. Pools, Foam and Iris each have a different way of arranging shape, texture and colour.

Your choices stay yours

No account, tracking analytics or public feed. A temporary cookie keeps your exploration together for 30 minutes of inactivity, up to one day. A server restart may end that session.

Favourites are optionally saved as small recipe records in this browser. Blocked storage, private browsing or clearing browser data may remove them. Download your images or export recipes to keep your own copy.

Generated images are disposable cached files, available for up to 24 hours and removed sooner when space is needed. Expired files are cleaned up periodically. Downloading keeps a copy on your device.

Artwork and palette provenance

Artworks are generated by go-graphics. Colour directions draw from the repository's ColorLisa palette data: Richard Diebenkorn, Pavel Tchelitchew, Edward Hopper and Paul Cézanne. These are generated examples, not reproductions of those artists' paintings.

Local review edition. Public usage terms, artist approval and operator contact must be settled before launch.
