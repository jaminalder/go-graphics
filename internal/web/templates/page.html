Made with a little chance.

Singular Seed creates original generative images. Pools, Foam and Iris each have a different way of arranging shape, texture and colour.

Choose a direction, find an image you love, and follow it somewhere new.

Artwork and colour

Artworks are generated by go-graphics. Colour directions draw from the repository’s ColorLisa palette data: Richard Diebenkorn, Pavel Tchelitchew, Edward Hopper and Paul Cézanne. These are generated examples, not reproductions of those artists’ paintings.

No account, tracking analytics or public feed. Download an image to keep your own copy.

Local review edition. Public usage terms, artist approval and operator contact must be settled before launch.
